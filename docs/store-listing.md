**Full description (≤4000), EN:**

Talk naturally with anyone, in two languages, on one phone.

Voice Realtime Translater listens to a conversation, detects which language each person is speaking, and instantly shows and reads the translation in the other language. No need to tap a language button every time someone speaks.

WHY PEOPLE USE IT
• Automatic language detection: speak English or Vietnamese (or any pair you choose) and the app translates to the other one.
• Two-way conversation view: each speaker's lines appear on their own side, like a chat.
• Male / female voice labels: each sentence is tagged by voice pitch so you can follow who said what. Tap a label to correct it.
• Read aloud: the translation is spoken with a matching male or female voice.
• Private by design: speech recognition and translation run on your phone. Your conversations are not uploaded.
• Works offline: after a one-time download of the speech model and language packs, no internet is needed to translate.
• History with search, pin, rename and replay of the original audio.
• Import / Export: back up everything as a .zip (with audio), export transcripts as .txt or subtitles as .srt, and restore on a new phone.
• Light and dark themes, app available in English and Vietnamese.
• Works on phones, tablets and foldables, with a side-by-side layout on large screens.

GREAT FOR
Travel, hotel check-in, taxis and airports, meetings with foreign partners, customer service, language practice, and talking with family abroad.

SUPPORTED LANGUAGES
English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Indonesian, French, Spanish, German and Russian. Choose any two as your conversation pair.

FREE WITH ADS
The app is free and supported by ads. Ads never interrupt a recording; a full-screen ad may appear only after you end a session. You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase.

Notes: translation quality depends on audio clarity and the language pair. Voice labels are estimated from pitch and may be wrong for children or some voices.
